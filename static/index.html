Question paper to slides

Upload a question paper as a PDF or as photos of the pages. You get a PowerPoint with one question per slide, which you can check and fix here before downloading.

    Add as many keys as you have. When one key reaches its free limit the next one is used automatically. Keys from different Google projects give more free requests (keys in the same project share one limit).

      Files are deleted from the server after processing. Results are deleted after a while, so download your PowerPoint when you are happy with it.
